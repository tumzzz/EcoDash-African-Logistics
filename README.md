EcoDash solar railway food cargo transportation
My mathematical model will use velocity, acceleration, trigonometry, and distance to work with movement and energy.
My platform is introducing a railway path for delivery with the use of solar power which can deliver food packages with battery for use charged up by solar power. 
This will help deliver the packages on time, and it is a quick process. 
The delivery process would not be affected even during loadshedding.
